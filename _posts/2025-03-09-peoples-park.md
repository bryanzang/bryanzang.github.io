People's Park is one of the many spots in downtown Chengdu where locals and tourists alike, both get to experience the leisure of the Chengdu soul. Over the years, it has transformed from a historical sight to a nature sanctuary and now in recent years, as well as a place for people to post 相亲帖 (blind dates). From the young to the elderly, everyone is free to enjoy this public space as the 辛亥秋宝路 (Railway Protection Movement of 1911) monument and its martys oversees the peace of present-day.
