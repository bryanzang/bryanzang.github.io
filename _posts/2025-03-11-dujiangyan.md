For those who don't know, the city of Dujiangyan is located about 70km away from the heart of Chengdu. It houses both the famous Taoist mountain 青城山 and also the historical Dujiangyan irrigation system that has the provided Sichuan plains with a continuous supply of fresh water via a dam-free, water-diversion design. This place is a symbol of history and culture marked by those from 2200 years ago.
