成都 Chengdu, quite literally "to become a capital" is and has been the name for this city for over 2500 years. With 4500 years of known  civilization and 2300 years of city building, Chengdu is undoubtedly one of the most interesting cities in the world. It is a living record of ancient Chinese history and one of the few that are situated in western China far from the central plains. There is a saying "少不入川" which is a jokingly way of describing how nice life can be here in 天府之国 (Heaven's Kingdom) -- so much so that young people soften their determination and seekage for hard work. Chengdu is a magnificent city, rich in culture, history, food, and of course also its people. It is a welcoming city, opening up its warm arms for those who come by. Stereotypes have stained its name in recent years, but it is inevitable in having the city be brought to an international stand-point, mouths will run words and one after another false images are built. Only first-hand experiences can truly and deeply let someone understand this city and its soul.
